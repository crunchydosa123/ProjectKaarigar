Here's a 3-part script for a
24-second short video, focusing on
the "Mundane Object, Aesthetic
Makeover" idea using a basic plastic
food storage container.

---

Video Title: Pantry Glow-Up

---

PART 1: The Mundane Reality (0-8
seconds)

Visuals:        [0-2s] Quick pan
over a slightly disorganized pantry
shelf, featuring a few generic,
mismatched plastic food containers
(some stained, maybe old takeaway
containers).        [2-5s] Close-up
on one particularly unappealing,
slightly yellowed or scratched
plastic container filled with
something dull (e.g., old oats,
half-eaten cereal).        [5-8s] A
hand picks up the container, looks
at it with a hint of unenthusiasm,
then sets it back down.    Audio:
[0-2s] Soft, slightly monotonous hum
or gentle clattering sound.
[2-8s] A subtle, almost questioning
synth pad sound; very light, non-
intrusive.    Text Overlay:
[3-8s] "Your everyday storage...
feeling a little bland?"
